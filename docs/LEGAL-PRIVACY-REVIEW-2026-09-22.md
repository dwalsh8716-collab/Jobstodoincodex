# Essential Resourcing Legal And Privacy Review

Review date: 22 September 2026

Scope:

- Privacy Policy
- Candidate Privacy Notice
- Cookie Policy
- Terms of Website Use
- Candidate and client website forms
- CV upload and private delivery
- Analytics consent
- Recruitment-agency website obligations

## Status

The public wording has been rewritten to reflect the website and recruitment
workflow that are actually live.

The policies are now materially stronger and clearer. They cover the main UK
GDPR transparency points, PECR cookie controls and relevant recruitment-agency
website principles without turning the site into a legal textbook.

This is a practical compliance review, not a solicitor's opinion. A qualified
UK adviser should still confirm the business identity, regulatory status,
contracts and operational decisions listed below.

## Changes Made

### Privacy Policy

- Identifies Essential Resourcing, operated by David Walsh, as controller.
- Explains categories and sources of personal information.
- States the purposes and lawful bases used.
- Names the current categories of service provider.
- Covers international processing and safeguards.
- Adds retention criteria and candidate review periods.
- Explains security, rights, complaints and automated decision-making.
- Confirms Sanity is public-content only.

### Candidate Privacy Notice

- Explains direct and indirect candidate-data sources.
- Separates core recruitment processing from optional consent.
- States that identifiable candidate information is not sent to a client
  without an appropriate conversation and permission.
- Covers CV storage, file checks, processors, international processing,
  retention, rights and complaints.
- Confirms there is no solely automated introduction or hiring decision.
- Updates the recorded notice version to
  `candidate-privacy-v2-2026-09`.

### Cookie Policy

- Describes the storage the website actually uses.
- Lists the two Essential consent-preference records.
- Lists the current GA4 cookies and default maximum duration.
- Confirms GA4 loads only after analytics consent.
- Confirms advertising and retargeting tags are not currently active.
- Explains that rejecting optional tracking does not restrict the website.
- Adds a direct control for reopening cookie preferences.
- Consent choices now expire after six months and must then be renewed.

### Terms Of Website Use

- Distinguishes website terms from client and candidate engagement terms.
- Explains the employment-agency role for direct permanent introductions.
- Confirms candidates are not charged for work-finding services.
- Covers genuine job advertising, applications, market content, intellectual
  property, third-party links, availability, liability and governing law.
- Avoids pretending that a website visit guarantees work, a hire or an offer.

### Forms

- Required privacy wording is now an acknowledgement of the recruitment
  processing described in the notice.
- Optional future-opportunity and WhatsApp choices remain separate.
- Marketing consent remains separate and must not be pre-ticked.

## Manual Decisions To Confirm

### 1. Legal identity and correspondence address

Confirm the legal status behind the trading name Essential Resourcing:

- David Walsh as a sole trader; or
- the full registered company or partnership name.

Add the legal name, company number if applicable, and a suitable correspondence
address to the Privacy Policy and Terms. Do not publish a home address if an
appropriate business or service address should be used instead.

### 2. ICO registration

Confirm whether the business is registered and the data protection fee is
current. Add the registration number to the policy if David wants it shown.

### 3. Employment agency or employment business

The website describes Essential Resourcing as an employment agency for direct
employment introductions.

Confirm whether any Fractional or interim arrangement makes Essential
Resourcing an employment business that engages or pays a worker. If so,
separate worker terms, Key Information Documents and Agency Workers Regulations
processes may apply.

### 4. Written recruitment terms

Website terms do not replace:

- client terms of business;
- candidate/work-seeker terms where required;
- assignment information;
- written confirmation of information supplied to work-seekers and hirers.

Review these documents against the Conduct of Employment Agencies and
Employment Businesses Regulations 2003.

### 5. Job advertisements

Before publishing a live role, confirm:

- the client has authorised the search;
- the full business name is clear;
- permanent, temporary, contract or Fractional status is clear;
- location, pay and required experience are accurate where stated.

### 6. Retention operations

The public commitment is:

- candidate records reviewed no later than 24 months after last meaningful
  contact;
- legally required recruitment-agency records retained for at least 12 months;
- longer retention only where an active relationship or legal reason supports
  it.

Create a recurring manual review until deletion automation is approved.

### 7. Processor and transfer records

Keep current contracts or data-processing terms for:

- Railway;
- Resend;
- Google Workspace, Calendar and Analytics;
- WhatsApp;
- any future CRM, ATS, AI or marketing provider.

Record where data may be processed and the applicable UK adequacy or
contractual transfer mechanism.

### 8. Legitimate interests

Keep a short legitimate-interests assessment covering:

- identifying and approaching potentially relevant candidates;
- retaining proportionate recruitment records;
- responding to client enquiries;
- website security and fraud prevention.

### 9. CV security

Private storage, file validation and manual review are live. Automated malware
scanning remains a worthwhile improvement. Keep CVs out of Sanity, analytics,
GitHub and public URLs.

### 10. Complaints and incidents

Maintain a simple internal process for:

- privacy complaints;
- rights requests and identity checks;
- suspected data breaches;
- documenting decisions and responses.
