- Official OpenAI Codex manual: best practices, `AGENTS.md`, in-app browser, automations and Sites guidance.
- Official Next.js 16 upgrade guide: Turbopack-by-default build path, React 19.2 support, current App Router upgrade expectations and AI-agent upgrade guidance.

## What Not To Overdo

- Do not force `npm audit fix --force` if it downgrades Next or Sanity.
- Do not publish draft proof, unverified salary data or fake case outcomes.
- Do not add CV upload until secure object storage, retention policy and legal wording are in place.
- Do not treat `llms.txt` as a replacement for real content structure, schema and internal links.
