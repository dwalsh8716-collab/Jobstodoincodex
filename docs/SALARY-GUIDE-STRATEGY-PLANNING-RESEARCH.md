The added figures are proposed regional planning estimates, not measured Manchester salaries or approved placement evidence. ## Scope

- Brand/creative planning and strategy: creative briefs, positioning and audience insight, not corporate strategy.
- Media strategy and specialist communications planning: separate role families within one table, not a single promotion ladder.
- Media client leadership/planning and buying coordination: integrated account responsibility, not media-owner sales or specialist activation.
- Marketing/media analytics: reporting, measurement and analytical leadership; excludes engineering, enterprise BI and data science.
- Consumer/audience insight: research projects, programmes and functions. Agency grades and client-side Heads are not a universal hierarchy.

The original Marketing / Digital Analyst row was moved unchanged out of PR/communications. All other original salary rows remain unchanged.
