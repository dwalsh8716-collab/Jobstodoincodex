# Brand, Copy And Tone Audit

Audit date: 3 July 2026

Status: strong voice, small sharpening pass completed.

## 1. Brand/Copy Executive Summary

The site already has a distinctive voice. It is not bland recruiter copy. The
strongest lines are plain, specific and commercially useful:

- No faff.
- No CV flinging.
- The job title is not the brief.
- Hiring done properly.
- Before you waste six weeks, talk to David.

That should stay.

The copy does the important jobs:

- explains who David helps
- prioritises hiring managers without ignoring candidates
- makes the founder-led model clear
- avoids fake proof
- explains what happens next
- keeps the tone human and direct

Safe improvements implemented:

- About Essential hero is now sharper and less dry
- generic card CTAs are more specific
- candidate "journey" phrasing is now plainer process language
- candidate form assurance now says "Private two-minute note"

No broad rewrite was needed. The site needs sharper proof over time, not a new
voice.

## 2. Weak Or Generic Copy Sections

Fixed:

- About Essential hero was accurate but a bit formal.
- Card links such as "View service" and "View role" were functional but flat.
- Candidate wording used "journey" where "process" was more natural.

Remaining watch points:

- Any new CMS content must avoid generic recruitment phrases.
- Salary content must not pretend certainty until data is validated.
- Case studies must not publish vague outcomes.

## 3. Strong Copy Worth Keeping

Keep:

- "No faff. No dickheads."
- "The job title is not the brief."
- "No CV flinging."
- "Proof has to earn its place here."
- "A senior brief should feel calmer after the first conversation."
- "Good roles. Honest advice. No recruitment nonsense."
- "Fastest way to reach me? Message me on WhatsApp."

These lines are memorable because they sound like a person, not a committee.

## 4. Tone Inconsistencies

No major live-site inconsistency was found.

Minor issue fixed:

- "journey" was toned down where it made candidate copy feel a touch producty.

Avoid in future:

- talent solutions
- submit vacancy
- request talent
- best-in-class
- unlock potential
- end-to-end recruitment partner
- dynamic team
- exciting opportunity

## 5. Missing Proof Points

The site is honest about proof. That is better than filling space with fake
logos or invented metrics.

Still missing:

- permissioned case studies
- verified outcomes
- named testimonials
- real salary snapshots
- reviewable Google Business Profile/review signals

Do not invent these. Add them only when David has permission and evidence.

## 7. Homepage Copy Improvements

Status: no major alteration needed.

The homepage is bold, memorable and commercially clear. It has:

- strong first impression
- clear founder-led positioning
- buyer/candidate split
- service clarity
- proof caveat
- final CTA

Recommendation: keep it. Improve with real proof later.

## 8. Service Section Improvements

Status: already strengthened by the SEO/CRO passes.

Service pages now explain:

- audience
- problem
- use case
- process
- mistakes
- proof standard

- FAQs
- next action

Recommendation: add more real examples over time, not more adjectives.

## 9. Candidate Section Improvements

Implemented:

- no-live-roles state now gives a clear confidential route
- candidate copy now uses plainer process language
- candidate form assurance is softer and more human

Keep the promise simple:

- no pointless CV sending
- no pushing people into wrong roles
- no CV upload until private storage is ready
- clear privacy route

## 10. CTA Improvements

Implemented:

- service cards now use `Explore the service`
- draft case-study cards now use `View proof standard`
- job cards now use `Read the role`
- contact hero now uses `Send the brief`

Recommended CTA set:

- Talk to David
- Sense-check a brief
- Send the brief
- Send a confidential note
- Message David on WhatsApp
- Book 15 minutes
- Discuss Strategic Interim

Final rule: sharpen the voice, do not sand it down. The edge is part of the
brand, but the buyer still needs to feel in safe hands.
