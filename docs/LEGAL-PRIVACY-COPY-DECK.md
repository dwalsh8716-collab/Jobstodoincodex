Privacy Policy

PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Privacy policies are rarely anybody's favourite read. This one explains, as plainly as possible, what Essential Resourcing collects, why it is needed and what control you have over it.

Last reviewed: 22 September 2026.

Who is responsible for your information?

Essential Resourcing, operated by David Walsh, is the data controller for personal information handled through this website and the recruitment services described here.

For privacy questions, complaints or rights requests, email david@essentialresourcing.co.uk. Candidates can also use the candidate data request form.

What information may be collected?

The information depends on why you contact Essential Resourcing.

Contact details such as your name, email address, phone number and preferred contact method.
Professional information such as your employer, role, LinkedIn or portfolio URL, employment history, salary expectations and CV.
Client information such as company details, hiring requirements, role briefs and messages you choose to send.
Recruitment records such as conversations, applications, interview feedback, introductions and placement information.
Technical information needed to keep the website secure and, where you consent, understand how the website is used.

Please do not send passwords, bank details or unnecessary sensitive information through a website form.

Where does the information come from?

Most information comes directly from you. It may also come from:

Clients discussing a role or recruitment process.
Public professional sources such as LinkedIn, company websites and published portfolios.
Job boards, professional networks, referrals or people who recommend an introduction.
Service providers used to operate the website, forms, email, bookings and analytics.

Where information is obtained from somewhere other than you, Essential Resourcing will provide appropriate privacy information when required.

Why is it used?

Personal information may be used to:

Reply to enquiries and arrange conversations.
Understand and manage client recruitment requirements.
Discuss roles and provide recruitment services to candidates.
Assess suitability and manage applications or introductions.
Keep accurate recruitment, consent and compliance records.
Operate, secure and improve the website.
Deal with privacy requests, complaints and legal obligations.
The lawful bases used

Data protection law requires a lawful basis for each use. Depending on the circumstances, Essential Resourcing relies on:

Legitimate interests to provide a relevant, properly run recruitment service, maintain professional records, protect the website and respond to business enquiries, where those interests are not overridden by your rights.
Steps before a contract or performance of a contract where you ask Essential Resourcing to provide recruitment or hiring services.
Legal obligations where records or action are required by recruitment, tax, data protection or other applicable law.
Consent for genuinely optional activities, such as non-essential analytics, marketing or keeping a candidate in mind where consent is the basis being used. Consent can be withdrawn at any time.
Candidate information

Candidate applications, CVs, introductions and recruitment conversations are explained in more detail in the Candidate Privacy Notice.

Candidate information is not sent to a client simply because it is sitting in a database. David will discuss a relevant opportunity and obtain the appropriate permission before making an identifiable introduction.

Who may receive information?

Information may be shared with:

Clients and prospective employers where there is a genuine recruitment purpose and the appropriate candidate permission.
Hosting, private file storage, email, calendar, analytics and other technology providers used to run the service.
Professional advisers, insurers, regulators, courts or public authorities where reasonably necessary or legally required.

Essential Resourcing does not sell personal information or hand it to unrelated businesses for their own marketing.

Current service providers

The website and recruitment workflow currently use providers that may include Railway for hosting and private CV storage, Resend for email delivery, Google Workspace and Google Calendar, Google Analytics where consent is given, Sanity for public website content, and WhatsApp where you choose to use it.

Sanity is used for public website content and must not be used to store private candidate CVs, application messages or recruitment notes.

International processing

Some technology providers may process information outside the UK. Where UK data protection law treats this as a restricted transfer, Essential Resourcing will rely on an applicable adequacy regulation or approved contractual and organisational safeguards.

How long is information kept?

Information is kept only while there is a genuine business, recruitment or legal reason for it.

Candidate records are reviewed no later than 24 months after the last meaningful contact, unless the relationship remains active, you ask to stay in touch or a legal reason requires longer.
Certain recruitment-agency records must be retained for at least 12 months under applicable conduct regulations.
Client, contractual, financial and compliance records may be kept for longer where required for legal, tax or dispute purposes.
Privacy request and complaint records are retained for as long as reasonably needed to show that the matter was handled properly.

Information that is no longer needed should be deleted or anonymised securely.

Security

Reasonable technical and organisational measures are used to protect information. CVs use approved private storage and delivery routes; they are not placed in the public website, analytics or Sanity.

No internet service is completely risk-free, but access is limited and the amount of information collected is kept proportionate to the job being done.

Analytics, cookies and external services

Google Analytics only loads after analytics consent. Advertising or retargeting tags are not currently active. More detail is available in the Cookie Policy.

If you use LinkedIn, WhatsApp or Google's booking interface, those services also process information under their own privacy terms.

Automated decisions

Essential Resourcing does not use solely automated decision-making to decide whether a candidate is introduced, interviewed or hired. Technology may help organise or search information. David's judgement remains involved in recruitment decisions made by Essential Resourcing.

Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, you may have the right to:

Ask for a copy of your personal information.
Correct inaccurate or incomplete information.
Ask for information to be deleted or restricted.
Object to processing based on legitimate interests.
Receive eligible information in a portable format.
Withdraw consent where processing relies on consent.

Rights are not absolute, and a request may be limited where the law permits or requires information to be retained. Identity may need to be checked before private information is released or changed.

Complaints

Please contact David first so the issue can be understood and put right where possible. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.

Changes to this policy

This policy will be reviewed when the website, recruitment workflow or service providers change materially. The review date at the top will be updated when that happens.

CANDIDATE PRIVACY

Your details should be handled properly.

This notice explains what happens when you contact David about a role, send your details or apply through Essential Resourcing.

Last reviewed: 22 September 2026.

Who is responsible for your information?

Essential Resourcing, operated by David Walsh, is the data controller for candidate information handled through its recruitment services.

David handles candidate conversations directly. Your information is there to support genuine recruitment work, not to be fired around the market hoping somebody bites.

What information may be collected?
Your name, email address and optional phone number.
Your LinkedIn profile, portfolio or other professional profile.
Your CV, work history, skills, qualifications, salary information, availability and location preferences.
Application details, recruitment conversations, interview feedback, references and notes relevant to a role.
Your contact preferences and records showing the privacy choices or permissions you have given.

Where might it come from?

Most candidate information comes from you. David may also identify or receive relevant professional information from:

LinkedIn, professional profiles and company websites.
Job boards and recruitment platforms.
Clients, referees and professional introductions.
Publicly available portfolios, articles or event profiles.

Why is it used?

Candidate information may be used to:

Respond to you and understand what you are looking for.
Assess whether a particular role may be relevant.
Discuss opportunities, salary and the market with you.
Manage applications, interviews, feedback and offers.
Make an agreed introduction and provide appropriate information to a client.
Keep accurate recruitment, consent and legally required records.
Keep you in mind for relevant future opportunities where there is an appropriate basis to do so.
The lawful bases used

Essential Resourcing normally relies on legitimate interests to run a relevant professional recruitment service, respond to candidates and maintain proportionate records. It may also rely on steps taken at your request before a contract, performance of a contract, legal obligations or consent where a choice is genuinely optional.

Agreeing to receive a WhatsApp reply or asking David to keep you in mind is separate from signing up to marketing. Essential Resourcing does not treat either choice as permission for broadcast marketing.

Sharing information with clients

Your identifiable CV or profile will not be sent to a client without a proper conversation and the appropriate permission for that introduction.

Where an introduction goes ahead, a client may receive information relevant to assessing you for the role, such as your CV, experience, salary expectations, availability and David's recruitment notes. The client becomes responsible for its own use of that information.

CVs and private files

CVs submitted through the website are stored in approved private storage and delivered privately to David. They are not put in the public website, Sanity, GitHub or analytics.

Uploaded files are checked for permitted format, file signature and size. David manually reviews candidate files before any client use.

Service providers

Candidate information may be processed using Railway for website hosting and private file storage, Resend and Google Workspace for email, Google Calendar for bookings, and WhatsApp where you choose that contact route. These providers act under their own terms and, where applicable, data-processing arrangements.

Some providers may process information outside the UK. Where this is a restricted transfer, an applicable adequacy regulation or approved safeguard must be used.

How long is information kept?

Candidate records are reviewed no later than 24 months after the last meaningful contact. They may be kept longer where the recruitment relationship remains active, you have asked David to keep you in mind, or a legal or regulatory reason requires it. You can ask David to delete your details at any time, subject to those obligations.

Certain employment-agency records must be kept for at least 12 months. A deletion request may therefore be limited where a record still has to be retained by law or is reasonably needed for a legal claim.

Automated decisions

Technology may help David search, organise or review professional information. Essential Resourcing does not use solely automated decision-making to decide whether you are introduced, interviewed or hired.

Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, you may ask for access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability of your information, object to processing based on legitimate interests, or withdraw consent where consent is being relied on.

Use the candidate data request form, or email david@essentialresourcing.co.uk. Identity may need to be checked before private information is released, changed or deleted.

Questions or complaints

Contact David first and he will try to sort it properly. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.

Message David on WhatsApp
Full website privacy information

The Privacy Policy explains website analytics, general enquiries, security and other service providers in more detail.

Cookie Policy

COOKIE CHOICES

Cookie Policy

The short version: essential site storage keeps things working. Google Analytics only loads if you say yes. Advertising trackers are not currently active.

Last reviewed: 22 September 2026.

What are cookies and similar technologies?

Cookies are small files stored by your browser. Websites can also use local storage, scripts, tags and similar technologies to remember choices or understand how a site is used.

The same privacy rules can apply to these technologies even when they are not technically cookies.

What this website currently uses
Essential preference storage

The website stores your cookie choices in your browser so it does not have to ask on every page. This is necessary to respect the choices you make.

essential.consent-preferences: records whether you accepted analytics or marketing technologies.
essential.analytics-consent: supports compatibility with the site's earlier consent setting.

These preferences are kept for up to six months, unless you clear them sooner or change your choice.

Google Analytics 4

Google Analytics helps David understand which pages and content are useful. It only loads after you accept analytics.

_ga: distinguishes visitors and may remain for up to two years.
_ga_<container-id>: maintains session state and may remain for up to two years.

The implementation uses IP anonymisation and must not send names, email addresses, phone numbers, CV filenames or enquiry text to Google Analytics.

Marketing technologies

Advertising and retargeting tags are not currently configured. If that changes, they will remain off unless you actively allow marketing technologies, and this policy will be updated.

Your choice

You can accept all, reject non-essential technologies, or choose analytics and marketing separately. Rejecting them will not stop you using the website, contacting David or applying for a role.

Cookie preferences

You can also delete cookies and local storage through your browser. If you clear the saved preference, the website will ask again.

Links to other services

The website links to services such as LinkedIn, WhatsApp and Google Calendar. Simply seeing a link does not give those services consent to set tracking technologies on this website. If you follow the link, the destination service may use its own cookies under its own policy.

Changes

This policy and the consent controls will be reviewed if new analytics, advertising, embedded media or similar technology is introduced.

Questions

Email david@essentialresourcing.co.uk or read the Privacy Policy for the wider picture.
