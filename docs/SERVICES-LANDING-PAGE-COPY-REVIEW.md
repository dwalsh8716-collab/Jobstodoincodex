# Services Landing Page: Copy and Layout Review

**Page:** https://essentialresourcing.co.uk/services  
**Purpose:** Editorial review draft. This records the current page and navigation so the copy can be revised to match the updated four service pages and “How Essential Resourcing Works” page. It does not change the website.

## Current Page Layout

1. Breadcrumb: Home / Services
2. Dark, two-column introduction: eyebrow and H1 on the left; introduction copy on the right.
3. “Find your route” section: short explanation followed by four linked route prompts, then a contact sentence.
4. Light section: four detailed service cards in a two-by-two grid on desktop. Each card is a link to its service page.
5. Dark location band: Manchester and North West positioning.
6. Contact CTA: “Need good people?” with contact and WhatsApp actions.
7. Structured data lists the four services.

## Review Notes for the Next Copy Pass

- The route selector and the following card grid both present the same four services. Decide whether the route selector still adds value or whether it repeats the service cards.
- Keep the four client products distinct: Permanent Recruitment, Retained Search, Fractional and Market Intelligence & Advisory. Specialisms belong elsewhere.
- Align each service card with the updated page for that service. Keep the card concise; leave the detailed service process on its own page.
- Consider how the landing page should direct people to **How Essential Resourcing Works** (`/how-essential-resourcing-works`) so users can understand the overall approach as well as choose a service.
- Review the menu label **Fractional Leadership** against the approved product name **Fractional** and choose one consistent label for the page and navigation.
- Preserve the Manchester-led, North West-rooted, UK-wide positioning if it remains approved.
- Keep the contact and WhatsApp routes visible and usable on mobile.
